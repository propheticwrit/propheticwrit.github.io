## 2026-06-11 09:56 EDT — How is it that we choose our memories of events? We have this selective memory w…

How is it that we choose our memories of events? We have this selective memory where we're able to parse out specific moments that become the highlights. When serious thought is put to an event we start to see more nuance, the highs and lows. Still when we reflect the first things that come to mind are those selective memories.

Those memories help shape our perception of the past and our place in it. Do we highlight the pain of the journey or the joy of its completion? Do we remember all those little grievances in a relationship or the moments of bliss?

We're able to reshape this over time. We tend to highlight those memories that help shape the narrative we want to tell ourselves. When jilted by a lover we want to see them in a negative light, and choose to remember all the reasons why we knew the relationship would fail. When in love we choose to see our partner in the best of lights, overriding all those negative interactions with moments of pleasure.

## 2026-06-11 17:03 EDT — How can we match conscious goals with subconscious motivations? The process of s…

How can we match conscious goals with subconscious motivations? The process of self concordance.

Do our intrinsic motivations match with the values we profess to adhere to? This seems to me to be the fundamental question when trying to ensure that our goals are in line with our subconscious motivations. We want to believe that we have this value set which we strive to adhere to in life, based on our sense of morals and beliefs. And yet all too often thoughts and actions do not match these purported beliefs.

If we are to match goals with these subconscious motivations we have to first listen to and validate these subconscious thoughts. It has been proposed that the steps to do so are to ask the question, let it incubate while thinking about something else, and then wait for that moment of inspiration, the aha. The validation of the aha moment is if it aligns with your fundamental beliefs.

But what if your fundamental beliefs are not in alignment with your subconscious?

# a trick of the mind

**Author:** daniel yon  
**Status:** reading  
**Started:** 2026-07-22

## 2026-07-22 09:03
Peoples of different polynesian islands were brought up to believe that their thoughts we theirs alone, and no business of others. It was strictly taboo to ask or speculate on what someone else was thinking. So when christian missionairies came and started telling them that their thoughts were unclean their natural reaction was to reject this. How is it that you can tell me what I am thinking?

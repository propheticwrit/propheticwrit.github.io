# Traversal

**Author:** Maria Popova  
**Status:** reading  
**Started:** 2026-06-01

## 2026-06-22 23:03
> The mind has a peculiar way of protecting the heedless heart from breaking — a way of damning an impossible love from flooding in through a bramble of reasons and rationalizations, persuading the possessed person that the abundant joy of the other's company, the creative and intellectual invigoration, the ecstasy of understanding flowing between the two, must be an undiscovered species of friendship or admiration, or some other unhazardous substance of affection. But against a force of joy strong enough, against an invigoration ecstatic enough, the dam eventually gives way and the uncontrollable rapids of eros rush in. That is how people of high intelligence and sensitivity, people of otherwise exceptional self-awareness, often fail to realize — refuse to let themselves realize — that they are falling in love.
